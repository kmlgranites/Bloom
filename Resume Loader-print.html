Behavior

Duration
24s default · tunable 10–45s
Covers the 20–30s engineering window. Falls back gracefully if jobs load earlier — header swaps straight to “All set!”.
Progress bar
Continuous fill + shimmer
Live % + “~Xs remaining”. Under 5s, copy switches to “Just a few seconds left” to avoid a misleading countdown to zero.
Stage checklist
4 stages · ticks at 25/50/80/100%
Pulsing cyan dot for the active stage, check for completed, dimmed for pending. Gives structural sense of progress beyond the bar.
Tip strip
4s cadence · 6 tips · independent loop
Privacy → value → feature preview. Converts the wait into onboarding and reduces perceived duration.
