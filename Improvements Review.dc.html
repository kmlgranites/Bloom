1 · Onboarding & profile saving

Spec for engineering

Skills fail to save, EEO submissions error out, and the captcha resets the form. The root causes are bugs. Design defines how each failure should look so users never lose work.

Eng owns: the save bug, the EEO error, and keeping captcha state.

2 · Match accuracy

Designed in prototype

Users saw "Strong match" on jobs where they were missing core skills, and stopped trusting the score.

  • Required skills card on every job: "4 of 5 matched", with ✓ and ✕ chips.
  • "Strong match" only shows when every required skill is present. Otherwise the card says "Good match · Missing 1 required skill".
  • Synonym note: "Node.js matched via 'NodeJS' on your profile".
  • "Have it? Add to your skills" jumps to the Skills section of the profile.
See it: Jobs tab. Miro has every skill, Linear is missing one, Coinbase is missing two. Eng owns: synonym matching and the label rule on the backend.

3 + 4 · City filter and specializations

Designed in prototype

Both live in the existing Job Preferences modal, so there's no new screen to learn.

  • Specialization chips under Job Function: Frontend, Backend, Full Stack, Mobile, DevOps / Cloud, QA / Testing. Pick several, or none to see all of Software Engineering.
  • Near a city search with a 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 mi radius.
  • The radius only applies to Hybrid and On-site jobs. With Remote only, the field is disabled and explains why. Work authorization still applies to all of them.
See it: Jobs → Match Preference. Turn on Hybrid to enable the city search.

5 · Application tracking

Designed in prototype

Users couldn't tell what happened after Apply, why an application failed, or that it was waiting on them.

  • Statuses with a live ticker: Queued → Submitting → Submitted. Needs you branches off Queued.
  • Failure reasons in plain language: "Employer requires US citizenship · 1 credit refunded".
  • The Needs you tooltip confirms that earlier answers are saved and that we'll also email the user.
See it: Applications tab (ShutterTech, Palantir). Eng owns: the Needs you email and keeping answers when an application resumes.

6 · Job content, date posted, eligibility

Designed in prototype

Wrong company details, cut-off descriptions, no posting date, and auto-apply firing on jobs the user can't legally take.

  • "Posted 2d ago" with a clock icon next to the company name.
  • Eligibility chips under the title, checked against the user's preferences: green when eligible, orange for citizenship, clearance, or location conflicts.
  • On a conflict, Bloom doesn't auto-apply and says why. The user can still apply themselves.
See it: Jobs tab: Datadog (on-site conflict), Coinbase (citizenship), Stripe (clearance). Eng owns: parsing fixes for company data and truncated descriptions, plus extracting the restrictions.
